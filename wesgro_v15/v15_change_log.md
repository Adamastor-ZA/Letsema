## 7. Arithmetic

- Required seats, target: 2,500,000 / 0.80 = 3,125,000 inbound seats.
- Seats in the year to November 2025: 3,816,797 two-way / 2 = 1,908,399 each way.
- Gap: 3,125,000 - 1,908,399 = 1,216,601, or 63.7% more ("roughly two thirds").
- Base case: 2,175,778 / 0.80 = 2,719,723; gap 811,324, or 42.5%.
- Implied seat growth, 2025 to 2035: (3,125,000 / 1,908,399) to the power 1/10 = 1.051, so 5.1% a year; base case 1.036, so 3.6%.
- Record: international two-way passengers rose from 1.71 million in 2015 to 3.1 million in 2024, about 6.8% a year (already in 2.2).
- Jobs: 300,000 / 257,000 = 1.17, so about 17% above 2023.
- Stakeholder tallies: 223 insights; demand 64 (23 High, 40 Medium, 1 Low); supply 68 (44, 24, 0); enabling 91 (48, 38, 5).
