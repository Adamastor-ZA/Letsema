## Claims to confirm before circulation

1. **Seat ceiling.** Demand-Side states a 1.8m inbound seat ceiling (pp21, 53). Its authors should confirm the basis against MIDAS route capacity of 3.82m two-way seats (p17).
2. **Melbourne growth.** Benchmark p9 puts Melbourne's ~4.3% in a 2023-24 growth column. Page 17 gives 4.3% as a forecast average for short-term arrivals to Australia. The deck labels it a forecast, and the authors should correct p9.
3. **Factbase figures.** Cruise calls, the 250-call target and other factbase figures need checking against the Wesgro/StratEcon report and the other primary sources.
4. **NZ return.** The basis of the ~NZ$55m figure is not stated (Benchmark p54). The deck shows bookings only.
5. **Infrastructure labels.** The authors should confirm the swapped category titles on pp27-29 and remove the placeholder text on p15.
6. **India and China units.** MIDAS uses passengers, bookings and travellers interchangeably. The deck treats all of them as two-way GDS-estimated journeys.
7. **Contract extension.** Wesgro should confirm whether notice under SLA clause 4.3 has been given. The clause expects notice one calendar month before the 31 October expiry.
8. **Document dates.** Timeline dates are cover months. Several files carry later revision dates, such as Demand-Side (1 Oct 2026) and the SME plan (9 Oct 2026).

## Cover note

The deck argues that the Western Cape is capture-poor on distribution and coordination and demand-constrained on price, safety and airline economics. It argues that the final report must act on both. Seven findings carry that answer, each resting on at least two deliverables.

Proposed tree changes for v5:

1. Split air access into winning routes and underwriting capacity on full routes.
2. Raise cruise to its own focus area.
3. Reword seasonality as "create green-season demand".
4. Merge brand equity into a findable, bookable province.
5. Add a price and value position by market.
6. Reword priority markets as "convert India and China hub traffic now".
7. Mark workforce and skills as untested.
8. Treat sustainability and resilience as a risk lens, not a workstream.

Decisions requested:

1. Agree the clause 4.3 extension.
2. Adopt the working answer and the seven findings.
3. Approve the tree changes.
4. Fund a rapid marketing audit and benchmark.
5. Broker interviews with airlines, SA Tourism, the City and Home Affairs.
6. Accept how the remaining gaps are bounded, folded in or parked.
