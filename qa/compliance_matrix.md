## Hard format rules

1. Technical and financial proposals must be submitted separately, as two distinct PDFs. The RFP states this twice (end of Section 4 and end of Section 5), which signals it is strictly enforced. No cost, rate or fee figure may appear anywhere in the technical PDF.
2. Technical Proposal Part 1 is capped at a maximum of 5 pages, excluding annexes.
3. CVs may be placed in an annex to the technical proposal (and criterion 3.1's requirement for 'documentation' makes the annex effectively mandatory).
4. The technical proposal must be structured in three named Parts: Part 1 Technical Approach, Methodology and Detailed Work Plan; Part 2 Management, Key Personnel and Staffing Plan; Part 3 Corporate Capabilities, Experience, Past Performance and 3 client references.
5. A proposal is 'complete' only if it contains all listed submission items: Technical Proposal, Cost Proposal, and Signed Offeror Representation of Transparency, Integrity, Social Responsibility.
6. The Offeror Representation of Transparency, Integrity, Social Responsibility must be signed.
7. At least three client references must be provided.
8. All cost information must be expressed in USD. No other currency may appear.
9. The cost proposal must be all-inclusive of profit, fees and taxes.
10. The total cost must not exceed the maximum allocated budget of US$40,000.
11. The budget must show major expense line items, with unit prices, quantities and total price for every line.
12. All items and services must be clearly labelled and included in the total offered price - nothing priced as an optional extra outside the total.
13. A budget narrative must explain the basis for the estimate of every cost element or line item.
14. Supporting information must be provided in sufficient detail to allow a complete analysis of each cost element or line item.
15. The payment schedule must be tied to deliverables, with no more than 10% allocated to the Inception report.
16. No additional costs may be added after award, and proposed costs may not be revised after submission unless CI expressly requests it - so the price must be a closed fixed price with a complete assumptions list.
17. Any tax savings achieved through refunds or minimisation must be reflected in the total cost.
18. At least three brand solution models/concepts must be developed and evaluated.
19. All four deliverables must be scheduled to the stated weeks after contract signing: week 2, week 5, week 10 (report AND presentation) and week 12.
20. Each deliverable is subject to CI's written approval and acceptance as its acceptance criterion.

## Clarification questions for Conservation International

Genuine ambiguities in the RFP text. These should be put to CI before
submission, in one consolidated request.

1. Does the 'max 5 pages (excluding annexes)' cap apply only to Part 1, or to the whole technical proposal including Parts 2 and 3? The cap is printed in the Part 1 row of the table, but Parts 2 and 3 have no stated limit, which would make the technical narrative effectively unlimited. Ask CI to confirm, and whether a cover page, table of contents and compliance cross-reference table count toward the 5 pages.
2. Is there any page limit, or any format specification (page size, minimum font size, margins, language), for the annexes? Annexes carry the reference list, CVs, work samples and the signed representation, so an undisclosed limit would be material.
3. The 'Level of Effort and Cost of Services' section ends mid-sentence: 'This assignment is expected to be largely desk-based with virtual...'. Ask CI to supply the complete section. It may specify a required level of effort, a prescribed number of consultant days, a required LOE table format, or constraints on virtual engagement that change both the technical and cost proposals.
4. The RFP as issued to us contains no submission deadline, no submission method or email address, no point of contact for questions, no deadline for clarification questions, and no proposal validity period. All are needed before the bid can be planned. Ask CI to confirm them in writing.
5. Part 1 asks for 'relevant services undertaken in the past five (5) years' while evaluation criterion 2.2 scores 'relevant services provided in the last three years'. Which window governs? We will build the annex to satisfy both, but ask CI to confirm which is scored.
6. Criterion 1.2 reads 'Demonstrate relevant experience to undertake the given assignment (5-10 years)'. Does this mean the firm must have 5-10 years of continuous track record in this field, or that cited assignments must fall within a 5-10 year lookback window, or that named key staff must each have 5-10 years of relevant experience? This is the highest-weighted technical criterion at 20 marks, so the reading matters.
7. The evaluation table jumps from criterion 2.2 to criterion 2.4 with no 2.3. The stated weights sum correctly to 70, so no weight appears to be missing, but ask CI to confirm there is no omitted criterion 2.3 that bidders are expected to address.
8. Criterion 2.2 requires 'samples of work delivered'. What form and volume of sample does CI expect - full report PDFs, extracts, redacted deliverables - and how will commercial confidentiality be handled? Confirm whether redacted extracts are acceptable and whether samples count against any page limit.
9. Is the presentation in Deliverable 3 to be delivered virtually? The assignment is stated as desk-based with no travel, but 'presentations may need to be made to different stakeholder groups as appropriate' is open-ended. Ask CI to confirm: virtual delivery, how many sessions are expected, which stakeholder groups, over what period, and how many rounds of adaptation follow before the final report.
10. The financial evaluation criterion assesses cost 'inclusive of any applicable tax, reimbursables and travel', yet Section 5 states the assignment is desk-based and does not require travel. Confirm that no travel or reimbursable costs are expected, so that showing zero is read as compliance rather than omission.
11. What is the contract currency and what are the payment terms? Costs must be quoted in USD, but confirm whether the contract and payments will be settled in USD, what payment terms apply after acceptance of each deliverable (for example 30 days), which party bears foreign exchange risk on a ZAR-based supplier, and whether CI requires any retention.

Specifically: will CI provide a tax exemption or withholding certificate, is withholding tax expected to be deducted from payments to a South African supplier, and how should South African VAT on exported services be treated in the all-inclusive price? This is material at a US$40,000 ceiling.
13. Will CI share the list of 15 prioritised emerging protected areas, and when?
