## 3. Changes from December to October

The brief speaks of eight changes. The deliverables and structure.md support the following list; the count depends on what is treated as one change.

Structural changes (three):

1. D7 "Close the Air Access and Cruise Capacity Gaps" split into FA07 "Win direct routes and add seats on full routes" and FA08 "Grow cruise calls and ports". Driver: MIDAS gave air access its own evidence base (route loads, hub connections, fares before seats), and the Infrastructure Report's cruise section (p110 to p112) concluded that cruise is a product-and-marketing lever running at about 40% of berth capacity, a different problem from airport capacity. FA08 is the only entry labelled New.
2. D9 "Lead Removal of Infrastructure, Visa and Safety Deficits" moved from theme 2.1 (supply) to theme 3.2 (governance), becoming FA19. Driver: the deficits that stakeholders, the Demand-Side barriers slide and MIDAS raise most often (bilateral air rights, visa processing, safety perception) are controlled nationally, so they belong with the escalation agenda rather than with provincial supply. The Infrastructure Report still listed D9 among the entries it addressed (p3), so the move post-dates September 2026.
3. FA20 "Develop workforce and service skills" added under theme 3.2 with a Flag disposition. Driver: the 300,000-jobs ambition (Inception p4) has no labour analysis behind it in any deliverable; skills constraints surfaced only as side mentions (Stakeholder p27, p39, p43, p44; Inclusion p51; Inception p8).

Rewordings that change scope (six, all labelled Reworded):

4. D2 "Develop the Seasonality and Spread Proposition" (December) or "Develop seasonality, spread and Mega events proposition" (March onward) became FA02 "Create green-season demand with MICE and events". Spread moved out to FA03 and FA10; MICE replaced "Mega events". Evidence: MIDAS June share 6.4% of international seats; ICCA meetings clustering May to November (Demand-Side p37); CTICC's June to August and December to January troughs (Stakeholder p45).
5. D3 "Facilitate Adoption Along the Path to Purchase" became FA03 "Make the whole province findable and bookable". The focus shifted from source-market platforms to provincial product supply being invisible and unbookable (Inclusion p28, p33, p34; Infrastructure p32, p62, p84, p95; Stakeholder p42).
6. D4 "Pivot to Growth Markets (China, India, ME, ASEAN)" became FA04 "Convert India and China hub traffic". The Middle East and ASEAN dropped out of the title; the lever narrowed from pivoting to converting the 101,000 passengers already flying via Dubai, Doha and Addis Ababa (Demand-Side p46, p48; MIDAS S6.1, S6.2).
7. D7 (air half) became FA07 "Win direct routes and add seats on full routes", the second product of change 1, with the lever restated from closing a capacity gap to the two MIDAS findings (frequency and gauge on full routes now; direct India and China routes later).
8. D14 "Ensure Sustainable Destination Management" became FA14 "Manage climate risk and sustainable tourism". Destination-management funding (LTOs, Circular 131) and dated climate losses (Robben Island R16m, Cape Nature closures, Meiringspoort) replaced a generic sustainability entry.
9. D16 "Review Institutional Arrangements (Wesgro, DEDAT, CTT)" or "(local, provincial and national)" became FA16 "Align institutional stakeholders across all three tiers", with the review of three named bodies widened to alignment across national, provincial and local government.

Refinement (one, labelled Refined):

10. D13 "Drive Community and SME Inclusion" became FA13 "Embed local destination bodies for community inclusion", carrying the Inclusion plan's finding that organisation, not distance or product, is the binding constraint in 11 of 20 communities and that a seller with a stake comes first (Inclusion p12, p30). The 24 September agreement kept this at concept level (structure.md Chapter 3 drafting notes).

Title-only rewordings not counted as changes: FA05 drops the market list from D5; FA17 writes "industry and government" for "Industry > Government (Public and Private)"; FA09, FA10, FA11, FA12, FA15 and FA18 are the December titles in sentence case. All six carry Validated.

Where the count is uncertain. Counting by disposition label, the eight non-Validated, non-Refined, non-Flagged entries are the six Reworded (FA02, FA03, FA04, FA07, FA14, FA16), one New (FA08) and one Moved (FA19): eight, which is the most likely basis for the brief's figure. Counting structural events plus scope rewordings as above gives ten (items 1 to 10), or nine if the FA07 rewording is folded into the split. Structure.md section 4.4 cites "summary deck slide 14 change 9" as the source for which levers sit outside the province, so the deck's own change list runs to at least nine numbered items and is not the eight-item list by disposition. The report should quote the deck's slide 14 numbering once the deck is in hand and not derive its own.
