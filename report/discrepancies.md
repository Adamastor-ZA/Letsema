# Figure reconciliation and discrepancy log

Unlocking Tourism Growth, final research report. 3 November 2026. Built from the verified evidence extracts of every deliverable and working analysis in hand, tested against every figure cited in `structure.md` (2 November 2026) and in sections 3 and 6 of `plan.md`.

**How to read this file.** Part A is the reconciliation table: one row per figure the structure document or the plan cites, plus the other figures found in the extracts that the report will lean on. Part B is the numbered discrepancy log in the house format. Nothing has been resolved by averaging; where two values exist, both are recorded and the value the report will use is stated with the reason.

**Citation convention applied.** Interim deliverables are cited with the date printed on the cover and the PDF page index, because no deck prints page numbers: Current State Diagnostic, Dec 2025; Inception Report, Nov 2025; Stakeholder Insights Report, Mar 2026; Global Benchmarking Report, Mar 2026 (cover prints "2026" only; month from the 24.03.2026 file name); MIDAS Aviation Market Assessment, Mar 2026, cited by section title because the Word file has no page numbers; Market Assessment Report, May 2026; Demand-Side Analysis, Jun 2026; Infrastructure Report (final, 114 slides; cover prints July 2026, file dated 22 September 2026); SME and Community Inclusion Plan (cover prints September 2026, file dated 9 October 2026); Skift memos by title, date and section. The two Infrastructure and Inclusion dates are themselves logged (B1, B2) because the brief's convention list names Jun 2026 and Oct 2026 for them while their covers print July and September.

**Precedence applied.** Interim deliverable first; then working analyses (Market Assessment Report, Skift memos); then Wesgro and government data cited through a deliverable; then benchmark and reference reports; then public sources. The deliverable wins over the 14 October deck and over `structure.md`. Four decisions confirmed on 2 November override the hierarchy where stated: the growth case runs from the Market Assessment's 2025 base of 1,498,829 to 2,175,778 (base) and 2,516,282 (accelerated) in 2035 with the structure document's market-group increments; 1.44m FY24/25 stays the recovery baseline; the comparator set is the Benchmarking Report's six; initiatives are I1 onwards and the phases are Activate 2027 to 2028, Expand 2029 to 2031, Sustain 2032 to 2035.

**Status vocabulary.** Confirmed: the figure is printed in a deliverable as cited. Confirmed by working analysis only: printed only in the Market Assessment Report or a Skift memo. Differs: the deliverable prints a different value, a different basis, or two values. Not in hand: no document in hand carries it.
