## One reading for Michael to settle

Wesgro's plan targets "800,000 to 1 million new international arrivals" over FY26 to FY30. That can be read two ways, and the report now gives both:
- **As a rise in yearly arrivals by 2030,** the target is 2.6 to 3.3 times the base path's gain of about 304,000 and twice the accelerated path's. No modelled path delivers it, which sharpens the case that growth is a supply programme.
- **As extra arrivals summed over the five years,** the way the plan counts its other five targets, the base path adds about 890,000 and meets it. That figure is computed by interpolating the model's 2025, 2028 and 2030 points.

The report states both, and an open item asks Wesgro which it means and from what base year. The summary says the answer decides whether Wesgro's own target needs a step change in seats.
