## Decisions taken in drafting

1. Growth case from the May Market Assessment's 2025 base of 1,498,829 to 2,175,778 (base) and 2,516,282 (accelerated); FY24/25 1.44m kept as the airport series and quarterly proxy (confirmed 2 November).
2. Jobs indicator set on Wesgro's ratio of two jobs per 100 international passengers, a public source, applied to both paths: about 30,000 in 2025, rising to about 43,500 (base) and 50,300 (accelerated) in 2035. Labelled a ratio-based projection; the 300,000 ambition is restated as a total-sector figure for the labour analysis to test (confirmed 2 November).
3. Comparator set: the Benchmarking Report's six; no outside destination was needed for a precedent line (confirmed 2 November).
4. Eighteen initiatives, I1 to I18 in one sequence; focus areas 1 and 5 merge in I1, and 16 and 17 in I15.
5. Leads are proposals for Steerco to confirm; six initiatives have a partner as the accountable lead (mobility, quality, flagship sites, community pilots, LTO funding and climate, skills).
6. No rand cost is estimated anywhere: Chapter 4 sets a funding route for each initiative and leaves the bands as open item 6.
7. Targets only where a source supports a level (the model, the Inclusion Plan's pilot count, the jobs ratio); elsewhere a direction or a milestone, marked proposed. Computed milestones (the 2031 base value and the accelerated path) are marked *.
8. Unsupported figures in the October one-line reasons are corrected in the text, not repeated: eleven routes above 80% (sixteen at 75% or more); 79 vessels against 83 calls; the 250-call plan held open; 70% by phone attributed to CapeNature.
9. Inclusion held at concept level; no Wesgro-funded hub and no trade-mission incentives; roles assigned in Chapter 4 only.
10. Seasonality: plan decision 6 (65% October to March, provisional) applied throughout, with the normalised 61.9% shown beside it in 5.3 for your decision.

## Top ten discrepancies

1. B6 Routes above 80%: eleven of 27 in MIDAS, not sixteen (high).
2. B9 Cruise: 79 vessels against 83 calls; no source for 250 (high).
3. B56 Seasonality: the 2025 table sums to 104.9%; normalised, October to March is 61.9%, not 65% (high, found 4 November).
4. B3 Model base year: Market Assessment 2025 base against Demand-Side 2024 base; same 2035 end points (high).
5. B8 The 2035 ambition: 2.8m (Inception), 5.7m (Diagnostic), 3m (G4J), 2.5m (reframed) (high).
6. B7 Melbourne's 4.3% is an Australia-wide forecast, not a Melbourne actual (high).
7. B10 The 1.8m seat ceiling: crossed between the 2028 and 2030 points; no seat table behind it (medium).
8. B12 and B27 101,000 hub journeys: the sum of two estimates; the tables sum to 97,579 (medium).
9. B18 "70% by phone" is CapeNature's reservation mix, not a provincial share (medium).
10. B19 CTIA upgrade: ~R11.3bn headline against R9.94bn itemised and R10.1bn in the Inclusion Plan (medium).

- The signed SLA, the Rwanda and South Africa recovery plans, the G4J document and Wesgro's FY26 to FY30 plan were not in hand; the plan's shapes follow the structure document's description of them.
- No initiative could be costed, and no return projection made, from the evidence in hand.
- No provincial tourism employment baseline exists in any deliverable; the jobs indicator is a ratio-based projection.
- The M&E toolkit workbook (Excel) and the foreword are outside this build.
- Chapter 2 and Chapter 3 run below their page budgets; they were not padded.
